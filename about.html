About Us

Independent advice, shaped by two decades in finance.

Rahali Corporation Pty Ltd was built on a simple idea: everyday people deserve the same calibre of financial advice as the wealthy get from private bankers — without the institutional strings attached.

“Most people don’t need a cleverer product. They need a plan they’ll actually stick to — and someone accountable for it.” Ranjit Das — Principal Adviser, Rahali Corporation
Principal Adviser

Ranjit has provided financial advice since 2003 and currently oversees more than $120 million in client assets on behalf of over 100 clients — from young professionals to executives approaching retirement.

Before establishing Rahali, Ranjit spent over two decades in corporate roles across Information Technology and Banking with multinational organisations globally — experience that shapes a practical, numbers-first approach to advice.

Our approach

Advice without the institutional allegiance.

Independent

Holding our own AFSL and ACL means we can source products from virtually any provider in the market — our recommendations aren’t limited to a parent company’s shelf.

Personal

We take the time to understand why you’re seeking advice and what “financially secure” actually looks like for you, before we talk about products.

Accountable

Every recommendation is documented in a formal Statement of Advice, and our fees and obligations are set out plainly in our Financial Services & Credit Guide.
