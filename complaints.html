Complaints Procedure

Not happy with something? We want to hear it.

We aim to provide the best advice and service at all times. When we fall short, we’re committed to resolving it quickly, fairly, and free of charge to you.

Tell us first

Call us, email us, or put your complaint in writing — or use the form below. We aim to acknowledge every complaint within 1 business day of receiving it.

We investigate (Internal Dispute Resolution)

We’ll review what happened, and where needed, ask you for more information. In line with ASIC’s requirements, we aim to provide our final written response within 30 calendar days of receiving your complaint. If we need longer, we’ll tell you why and keep you updated on progress.

Still not satisfied? Go to AFCA — free of charge

If you’re not happy with our response, or we haven’t responded within 30 calendar days, you can lodge a complaint with the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) — an independent external dispute resolution service that costs you nothing.
