Professional Services

Advice that covers the whole financial picture.

From growing your wealth to protecting it, planning retirement, and financing property — we bring it together under one roof, backed by a trusted referral network for everything else.

Wealth creation & preservation

Strategies tailored to your risk appetite and timeframe, including:

  • Capital-protected investments and 100% loan structures
  • Negative gearing strategies
  • Structured investment products

Retirement planning

Building and protecting your retirement income, including:

  • Superannuation strategy and consolidation
  • Account-based pensions and transition-to-retirement
  • Personal insurance (life, TPD, income protection)

Mortgage & credit broking

As holders of our own Australian Credit Licence, we arrange:

  • Home loans for owner-occupied and investment properties
  • Refinancing and debt consolidation
  • Commercial lending
  • Personal and car loans

Trusted professional referrals

Where advice sits outside our licence, we connect you with vetted specialists:

  • Accountants — tax returns, family trusts, SMSF establishment
  • Solicitors — estate planning and testamentary trusts
How we choose products

Independent, not tied to any one provider.

Because Rahali Corporation holds its own Australian Financial Services Licence and Australian Credit Licence, we're able to source products from virtually any provider in the market. We continually research providers across investments, insurance and lending, and only recommend products we believe are genuinely competitive and suitable for your circumstances.

Full detail on how we're remunerated for these recommendations — including any commissions or fees paid by product providers — is set out in our Financial Services & Credit Guide and in the Statement of Advice prepared specifically for you.

A note on fees

We don't believe in surprise costs. Before any advice is implemented, you'll receive a clear written explanation of:

  • Any upfront advice or brokerage fees
  • Ongoing service fees, if applicable
  • Commissions payable by product providers (if any)

Nothing proceeds without your informed consent.
