How we choose products

Not tied to any one provider.

Because Rahali Corporation holds its own Australian Financial Services Licence and Australian Credit Licence, we’re able to source products from virtually any provider in the market. We continually research providers across investments, insurance and lending, and only recommend products we believe are genuinely competitive and suitable for your circumstances.

Full detail on how we’re remunerated for these recommendations — including any commissions or fees paid by product providers — is set out in our Financial Services & Credit Guide and in the Statement of Advice prepared specifically for you.

A note on fees

We don’t believe in surprise costs. Before any advice is implemented, you’ll receive a clear written explanation of:

  • Any upfront advice or brokerage fees
  • Ongoing service fees, if applicable
  • Commissions payable by product providers (if any)

Nothing proceeds without your informed consent.
