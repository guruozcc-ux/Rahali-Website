Put your current, licensee-approved Financial Services & Credit Guide PDF in this
folder and name it FSCG.pdf (this is what fscg.html links to).

The FSCG is a regulated ASIC disclosure document covering your specific AFSL
authorisations, adviser details, fee/remuneration structure and dispute
resolution arrangements. It must be prepared or reviewed by your AFSL
compliance function — do not generate its substantive content from a
website redesign. The version already published at
https://www.rahali.com/files/pdf/FSCG.pdf can be reused as-is if it is
still current, or replaced with an updated one.

Once uploaded to Zoho Sites' File Manager, update the link in fscg.html
(or the pasted HTML block in Zoho) to point at the uploaded file's URL.
