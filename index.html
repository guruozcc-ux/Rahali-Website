About the practice

Rahali Corporation Pty Ltd has been advising Australians on wealth creation, retirement and risk since 2003. Before founding the practice, Principal Adviser Ranjit Das spent two decades in corporate technology and banking — experience that shapes a style of advice that favours clarity over complexity.

The practice holds its own Australian Financial Services Licence and Australian Credit Licence, and works with a small, established panel of product and lending partners rather than a single institution’s shelf.
